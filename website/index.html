What you can do

Small help, big welcome.

Built for the people who need a neighbour most: newcomers finding their feet and elders who'd love a hand or a chat.

🤝

Community help board

Post a request: a form to fill, a ride to the clinic, groceries upstairs. A neighbour nearby offers to help.

💬

Language exchange

Practise French, share your language. Join conversation circles and language parties around Montréal.

📍

Find your community

Filter groups by neighbourhood, language and need: newcomer support, senior networks, conversation circles.

Honest ratings

How it works

Three steps to a friendlier city.

Join & agree

Sign up as a newcomer, elder or helper and accept the community rules: respect, privacy, no abuse.

Ask or offer

Post what you need or browse open requests near you. Accept one and start chatting.

Help & rate

Mark it complete and rate each other. Good neighbours build a trusted profile over time.
